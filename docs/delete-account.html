◇ DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT && DATA

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and saved data for Path of Boredom, published by MadBone (package com.madbone.pathofboredom).

Delete your account in the game (recommended)

Path of Boredom provides an in-game account deletion feature. This is the fastest way to permanently remove your account and associated data.

  1. Open Path of Boredom.
  2. Navigate to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request.

Once confirmed, your account and associated data are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Alternative: request deletion by email

Send an email to: bogdanmadose@gmail.com

  1. Use the subject line "Delete my Path of Boredom account".
  2. If possible, send the request from the same email address associated with your game account.
  3. State whether you want full account deletion or only your leaderboard entries removed.

Requests are handled manually, so please allow up to 30 days for processing. A confirmation email will be sent once the deletion has been completed.

What gets deleted

You can request either a full deletion or a partial deletion.

Full account deletion

Everything associated with your account is permanently removed:

Data Result
Your Google or Apple account identifier used for sign-in Permanently deleted
Your display name and email address Permanently deleted
Your saved game data, including character progress, levels, equipment, upgrades and campaign progress Permanently deleted
Your leaderboard entries and recorded scores Permanently deleted

Partial deletion (keep your account)

If you only want to be removed from public leaderboards but continue using your account, you may request deletion of your leaderboard entries while retaining your saved game progress.

How long data is kept

Once a deletion request is completed, the data is removed from the live server immediately. No archived copy is retained and the project does not keep long-term backups of player accounts.

If you do not request deletion, your account data may remain stored for as long as your account exists or until the project is discontinued.

Deleting the app is not the same as deleting your account

Uninstalling Path of Boredom removes the application from your device but does not automatically remove server-side account data. To permanently delete your account and saved data, use the in-game Delete Account feature or submit an email request as described above.

Google and Apple Sign-In

Path of Boredom supports sign-in through Google and Sign in with Apple. Deleting your account removes data stored by Path of Boredom, but does not delete your Google Account or Apple Account.

If you signed in with Google, you may separately revoke the game's access at Google Account Permissions .

If you signed in with Apple, you may manage or revoke access from your Apple ID settings under Sign in with Apple.

Revoking access through Google or Apple prevents future sign-ins but does not itself delete data already stored by Path of Boredom. To delete stored game data, use the account deletion methods described on this page.
