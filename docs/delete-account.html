Delete your account and data

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and saved data for Path of Boredom, the Android game published by MadBone (package com.madbone.pathofboredom).

How to request deletion

Send an email to: bee.fly961@eagereverest.com

  1. Use the subject line “Delete my Path of Boredom account”.
  2. Send it from the same email address you used to sign in to the game. This is how your account is identified, so a request sent from a different address cannot be matched to your data.
  3. State whether you want everything deleted, or only your leaderboard entry (see the options below).

Requests are handled manually by one person, so please allow up to 30 days. You will receive a confirmation by email once the deletion is complete.

What gets deleted

You can ask for either of the following.

Full account deletion

Everything associated with you is permanently removed:

Data Result
Your account identifier from Google or Apple Permanently deleted
Your display name and email address Permanently deleted
Your saved game (character, level, equipment, upgrades, campaign progress) Permanently deleted
Your leaderboard entries and recorded scores Permanently deleted

Partial deletion (keep your account)

If you only want to be removed from the public leaderboard but keep playing, you can ask for your leaderboard entries to be deleted while your saved game is kept. Say so in your email.

How long data is kept

Once a deletion request is carried out, the data is removed from the live server immediately. There is no additional retention period and no archived copy is kept, because the game does not keep long-term backups.

If you do not request deletion, your saved game and leaderboard entry are kept until you ask for them to be removed, or until the project is discontinued.

Deleting the app is not the same as deleting your account

Uninstalling the game removes it from your device but does not delete the saved game held on the server, because that data is attached to your sign-in account rather than to the device. To remove server-side data you must send the email request described above.

Separately, you can revoke the game's access to your account at any time in your Google account settings. This stops future sign-ins but does not by itself delete data already stored.
