Privacy Policy for Path of Boredom

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Path of Boredom ("the game") is a hobby game developed by MadBone. This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and who it is shared with. It applies to the Android app (com.madbone.pathofboredom) and to the browser version of the game.

Summary

The game does not show ads, does not contain analytics or tracking SDKs, does not profile you, and does not sell or share your data with any third party for advertising. The only personal data collected is what is needed to sign you in and keep your saved game attached to your account.

What the game collects

Data Why
An account identifier from your sign-in provider (Google, or Apple where offered) To identify which saved game belongs to you. This is an opaque identifier issued by the provider. The game does not receive or store your password.
Your display name Shown in the game and next to your score on the in-game leaderboard, so runs are attributable to a player.
Your email address, if your sign-in provider supplies it Used only to recognise the same person across sign-in methods so one player keeps one save. It is not used for marketing, and the game does not send you email.
Game progress and run data Your character, level, equipment, upgrades, chosen skills, score and progress through the campaign, so a run can be resumed and ranked. This is game data, not information about you personally.

In the desktop/office browser version, the game instead uses your Windows sign-in name to identify your save. That version is intended for use on a private network.

What the game does not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising networks are present in the app.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files or other device content.
  • No passwords. Sign-in is handled entirely by Google or Apple.

How the data is stored

Saved games and leaderboard entries are stored on a server operated by the developer, as ordinary files. Account identifiers are stored in hashed form where they are used to name those files. Data is sent between the app and the server over HTTPS.

This is a small hobby project rather than a commercial service. It is run on a best-effort basis and you should not rely on it to preserve data indefinitely. Saved games may be lost through server failure, maintenance or discontinuation of the project.

Who the data is shared with

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising or marketing purposes with anyone. Data is shared only with:

  • Google (and Apple, where that sign-in method is offered) — only as part of the sign-in process you initiate. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Other players — your display name, score, class and the build used for a ranked run are visible on the in-game leaderboard. Nothing else is shown publicly. Do not sign in with an account whose display name you are not willing to show there.

Data may also be disclosed if required by law.

How long data is kept

Your saved game and leaderboard entry are kept until you ask for them to be deleted, or until the project is discontinued.

Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask for your saved game, leaderboard entry and account identifier to be deleted, or ask for a copy of the data held about you. Requests are handled manually, so please allow a reasonable amount of time. Contact details are below.

You can also stop the app from holding further data at any time by signing out and uninstalling it, and you can revoke the game's access from your Google or Apple account settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children and is not designed to collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please get in touch and it will be removed.
